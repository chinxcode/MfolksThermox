About us

With roots tracing back to 1958 we come from one of India's oldest and most respected
business groups in the aluminium and copper ecosystem. At MFolks we've always believed in
growing alongside the industries we serve. Over the decades we've built deep expertise and
today we're channeling that experience into a space that's changing the way surfaces are
protected and restored Thermal Spray.

Our Manufacturing Units are located in the industrial township of Bhiwadi and Chopanki in
the state of Rajasthan and Depot in the industrial township of Narol, Ahmedabad in the state
of Gujarat in INDIA. Our units are spread over an area of 12000 sq. mtrs. having an installed
capacity to manufacture app. 30,000 M.Ts. of Aluminium Products per annum in different
grades & sizes and 6,000 Km of LT Power Cable per annum in different sizes.

Our goal is simple to create spray wires that last longer, perform better and keep your
equipment running reliably. Whether it's corrosion protection, part restoration or surface
strengthening, our thermal spray wire solutions are made to deliver and made to last. We o&
ffer a wide range of aluminium, zinc and alloyed wires for both Kflame and arc spray systems.
Every wire is crafted with a focus on precision, quality and consistency.

What truly sets us apart is how closely we stay connected to real world industry challenges.
In sectors like power, marine, oil & gas, automotive and heavy engineering, we know that
downtime is costly. That's why our wires are engineered not just for performance, but for
trust. You can rely on them for smoother coatings, better adhesion and reduced maintenance
over time.

At MFolks we're more than just manufacturers. We're listeners, collaborators and problem
solvers. As the demand for advanced surface technologies grows we're here to support you
with dependable products, technical clarity and a commitment to helping you succeed.
